How it works

Four steps. One clear incident.

01 — REPORT

Say what you see.

Describe it by text or voice, or attach an image.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Structured, not vague.

AI, or local fallback when AI is unavailable, extracts the category, urgency and observations.

03 — LOCATE

Shown on the map.

With your permission, your location appears directly on the LIFELINE map with its accuracy.

04 — COORDINATE

You confirm first.

Review the incident, then share it through an available communication channel.
